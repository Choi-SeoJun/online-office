# Korean weather region data

Source: Korea Meteorological Administration (기상청), **기상청41_단기예보 조회서비스_오픈API활용가이드_격자_위경도(2610).xlsx**, sheet `최종 업데이트 파일_20260930`, downloaded 2026-11-13 from the [official public data portal](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15084084/openapi.do). Portal attachment `FILE_000000007732424`, detail `1`, ZIP SHA-256 `9dfd7dc2d422318154d3484fb00142afa3c5271fc14d079adf997043a14b86ba`.

The portal permits reuse with source attribution (Public Nuri Type 1, as indicated on its listing). Visible attribution is included in the weather panel. These are **region representative points**, not surveyed boundaries or exact local observations. Current precipitation now uses KMA observations; see [KMA weather](kma-weather.md). Open-Meteo is the explicitly labelled fallback.

`game/korea-region-points.ts` retains the older city-level selections and two-decimal coordinates for saved preferences and installed clients. Its synthetic city points average non-autonomous districts. `game/korea-local-points.ts` adds 3,836 official points including 3,565 읍·면·동 points, six-decimal representative coordinates, and the original integer `nx`/`ny` grid. Generate it with `python scripts/generate-korea-local-points.py path/to/kma-2610.zip` (Python + openpyxl); the generator checks the source ZIP hash above.

The hierarchy preserves synthetic city IDs, splits `천안시서북구` into `천안시 → 서북구`, and attaches neighborhoods to their official districts. Metro cities go directly to their constituent 구/군, counties go to 읍/면, and Sejong skips its duplicate city level. Province, city and district representative choices remain available; selecting a parent clears its former descendants. Marine-only Ieodo is excluded.

The 2026 source includes **전남광주통합특별시**, established on 2026-07-01 ([official city site](https://www.jeonnam-gwangju.go.kr/)). It has a direct representative selection and optional constituent cities/counties/districts. Its broader area must not be silently collapsed to the former Gwangju footprint. Older Gwangju preference maps to the former city area's Seo-gu representative point. Other known old domestic choices migrate; foreign preferences fall back to Seoul.

Opt-in geolocation requests a fresh high-accuracy device position and computes the haversine-nearest **neighborhood** representative point locally. Province/city centers are not candidates. No raw device coordinates are stored or sent. A broad regional eligibility envelope prevents far-away foreign locations from selecting a Korean point; it is not a legal-border geofence. The selected domestic name is always visible, and manual selection is available. Only its known administrative ID goes to the server, which resolves the official grid; no arbitrary GPS coordinates or client-supplied grid is accepted. Legacy city-coordinate requests still work. Browser forecast caches use region IDs, so rounding cannot alias nearby neighborhoods. Upstream observation requests share work for the same KMA grid and publication cycle. Room payloads remain unchanged.

These are approximately **5 km forecast/observation grids**, not street-level sensors or administrative boundary polygons. Neighboring neighborhoods can share one grid. A nearest administrative point need not be the device's legal address, and device accuracy and publication delay still affect the result. The weather panel explains grid granularity.
